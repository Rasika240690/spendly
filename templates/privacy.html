Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the categories, amounts, descriptions, and dates you enter.
  • Session data — a session cookie that keeps you signed in.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers, or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store and display your expenses and spending summaries.
  • Keep the service secure and working properly.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and is only accessible through your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when fonts are requested. We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your account and data deleted, contact us at support@spendly.example.
